Our Heritage & Passion

Baking Happiness Into Every Single Slice

"We believe great baking starts with honesty — real butter, unbleached flour, pure Belgian cacao, and time."

Established at Haji Street, Moti Vorvad, Himmatnagar, Creme & Crumbs was born from a deep love for European patisserie and traditional warmth. What started as handcrafted custom birthday cakes has blossomed into Himmatnagar’s beloved neighborhood bakery serving warm flaky croissants at sunrise, comforting double-chocolate chunk cookies, velvety tiramisu, and show-stopping celebration cakes for life’s most precious milestones.
